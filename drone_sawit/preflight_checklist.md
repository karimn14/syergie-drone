# Preflight Checklist

1. Battery charged and installed
2. Propellers secure and undamaged
3. Pixhawk: Firmware loaded, parameters checked
4. Jetson: All services running (`main.py`, dashboard, etc.)
5. Arduino: Sprayer test passed
6. GPS lock acquired
7. Mission plan uploaded and validated
8. Geofence and failsafe configured
9. Safety pilot present
10. Emergency procedures reviewed
